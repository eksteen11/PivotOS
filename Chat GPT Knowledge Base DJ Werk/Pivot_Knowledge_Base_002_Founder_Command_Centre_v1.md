# Pivot Knowledge Base
# Capability 002 — Founder Command Centre (Executive Operating System)
**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Approved Foundation Specification

---

# Purpose

The Founder Command Centre is the central operating system for the founder.

It is not a task manager.

It is the place where strategy, decisions, communication, execution and long-term vision come together.

Its purpose is to maximise the founder's leverage every day.

---

# Vision

Pivot OS should not help the founder do more work.

Pivot OS should help the founder achieve more by making better decisions, reducing friction and allowing an AI workforce to execute the majority of operational work.

# Core Design Decisions

## Universal Inbox

Every piece of information enters through one intelligent inbox.

Examples:
- Voice
- Email
- WhatsApp
- Phone calls
- Documents
- Photos
- Meetings
- Payments
- Leads
- Ideas

Humans never decide where information belongs.

AI classifies, routes and links everything automatically.

---

## Founder Dashboard

The founder should immediately see:

- Three highest-leverage actions
- Biggest bottleneck
- AI work completed overnight
- Items requiring approval
- Goal progress
- Revenue overview
- Upcoming meetings
- Risks
- Opportunities

The dashboard should never become cluttered.

---

## Voice First

Voice is the primary input.

The founder should be able to naturally speak:

- ideas
- reminders
- meetings
- payments
- tasks
- thoughts

AI extracts structured information automatically.

---

## Communications Hub

Pivot should consolidate:

- Gmail
- WhatsApp
- Calls
- Social media notifications

AI identifies what actually needs the founder's attention.

---

## Venture Studio

Every idea becomes a living venture.

AI automatically scores ideas using:

- Revenue potential
- Strategic fit
- Time to MVP
- Capital required
- Technical complexity
- Reuse of existing assets
- Recommended priority

---

# Founder AI

The Founder Agent is NOT an assistant.

It is a proactive executive.

Every morning it should:

- Review every company
- Review every goal
- Review communications
- Identify bottlenecks
- Recommend today's priorities
- Prepare the day
- Suggest improvements
- Track execution
- Learn from outcomes
