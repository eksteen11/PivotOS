# Project Vision

Pivot OS is **not** another CRM, ERP or task manager.

It is a **Founder Operating System** that coordinates an AI workforce to help build and operate multiple businesses.

Its purpose is to:

- Maximise founder leverage.
- Increase revenue and profit.
- Reduce manual work.
- Continuously improve itself.
- Help build new companies.

The philosophy is:

> **Complex inside. Effortless outside.**

1. Humans create.
2. AI organises.
3. AI prepares.
4. Humans approve important decisions.
5. AI executes safe operational work.
6. AI measures outcomes.
7. AI improves continuously.

---

# The Three Immutable Laws

## Law 1

Capture everything.

Voice, email, WhatsApp, meetings, documents, ideas, payments, photos, leads and notes should all enter Pivot effortlessly.

## Law 2

AI organises everything.

Humans should never manually decide where information belongs.

## Law 3

Maximise founder leverage.

Every feature must answer:

"Will this help the founder create more value?"

# Universal Inbox

One of the biggest architectural decisions.

Everything enters through one universal capture layer:

- Voice
- Email
- WhatsApp
- Calls
- Meetings
- Documents
- Photos
- Payments
- Leads
- Ideas

The Inbox is **not** a work list.

It is an AI processing queue.

AI automatically classifies, links and routes information.

# Current Methodology

For every capability:

1. Founder explains current workflow.
2. AI asks clarifying questions.
3. Current workflow documented.
4. Bottlenecks identified.

6. AI workforce designed.
7. Capability specification written.
8. Save Markdown file.
9. Move to next capability.
