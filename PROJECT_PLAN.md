# PivotOS Project Plan

## Product Goal
Build a secure, deployed, fully navigable Founder Operating System for DJ that demonstrates every core business workflow using real knowledge-base context, a cloud-hosted mock database, simulated integrations, and approval-gated mock AI.

## First Release
- One private founder account with visibility across every entity and capability.
- Global Command Centre as the default home.
- Universal Inbox, AI recommendations, approvals, risks, priorities, revenue, and active work.
- Persistent cloud demo data with safe reset and reseed controls.
- Responsive feature parity across desktop, tablet, and mobile.
- Real business context may be used; sensitive external, legal, and financial actions remain simulated.

## Included Workspaces
1. Founder Command Centre and Manage Meetings
2. Northpoint Realty
3. Farm Feed
4. Digikraal Livestock Agency
5. Digikraal Property Investment
6. Partner Farming and Trading
7. Strategic Growth and Capital
8. Finance, Treasury, Accounting and Administration
9. Project Incubator and Opportunity Lab
10. Digital Product, Marketplace and Web Development

## Core Working Flows
- Founder: daily priorities, Universal Inbox, AI recommendations, approvals, activity, risks, and business health.
- Commercial: leads, supply and demand, matching, listings, deals, documents, stages, and follow-ups.
- Finance: invoices, bills, payments, treasury, reporting, reconciliation, and approval gates.
- Ventures: ideas, scoring, governance, capital decisions, projects, releases, and outcomes.
- Meetings: preparation, simulated transcription, decisions, tasks, and follow-up.
- Integrations: realistic simulated WhatsApp, Gmail, Calendar, Xero, Sage, banking, property, marketplace, and development connectors.

## AI Safety Model
AI may analyse, classify, draft, recommend, and prepare actions. Every external communication, payment, legal commitment, public publication, deletion, permission change, and final commercial transition requires DJ's approval. All execution is simulated and logged in the first release.

## Build Plan
1. Foundation: secure authentication, founder permissions, cloud demo schema, seed/reset controls, responsive shell, and sidebar navigation.
2. Founder Core: Global Command Centre, Universal Inbox, Founder Agent, approvals, meetings, activity, and simulated connectors.
3. Business Operations: all commercial workspaces, pipelines, matching, records, documents, and cross-workspace linking.
4. Finance and Growth: finance workspace, venture governance, opportunity lab, product delivery, reporting, and business health.
5. Completion: end-to-end stateful demo flows, responsive testing, privacy checks, deployment, and final UX polish.

## Definition of Done
- Every listed workspace is navigable and populated with coherent cloud demo data.
- All four core workflow groups are stateful and work end-to-end.
- Simulated AI and integrations clearly show pending, approved, completed, and failed states.
- Founder-only access and entity isolation are verified.
- Desktop, tablet, and mobile provide equivalent core capability.
- Build, lint, and critical user-flow tests pass.

## Out of Scope for First Release
- Real payments, bank releases, contracts, public posts, emails, or WhatsApp sends.
- Unapproved scraping or hidden browser automation.
- Replacing Xero, Sage, Propverse, Property24, Jira, or the Digikraal marketplace.
- Multi-user production access.
